About LBC Home Safety

Practical home safety and protection solutions across Patna, Kolkata, and selected areas of Bihar.

Dedicated to Safe & Protected Living Spaces

LBC Home Safety is a home safety and protection service provider with our primary office in Patna, Bihar, and operational coverage extending to Kolkata, West Bengal, as well as selected Bihar regions including Muzaffarpur, Danapur, Darbhanga, and Vaishali.

Modern residential architecture prioritizes wide balconies, large windows, and open terraces. While these spaces provide excellent natural sunlight and cross ventilation, they also require reliable safety barriers to prevent accidental falls and keep balconies clean from unwanted bird disturbances.

Our Core Solutions

We provide four essential safety and netting installations:

  • Invisible Grill Installation: Slim, high-tensile stainless steel wire systems for balconies and windows that ensure robust fall protection while maintaining unobstructed views.
  • Bird Net Installation: Transparent copolymer netting that effectively prevents pigeons and birds from roosting or fouling balconies and open ducts.
  • Safety Net Installation: Heavy-duty, tightly anchored safety netting engineered to protect children, elderly family members, and pets from accidental falls.
  • Sports Net Installation: Resilient netting enclosures designed for cricket practice pitches, football setups, badminton courts, and multi-sport enclosures.

Service Locations & Reach

LBC Home Safety serves customers across Patna and selected regional hubs including Danapur, Muzaffarpur, Darbhanga, and Vaishali. We also handle residential and commercial projects in Kolkata, West Bengal.

Every installation is performed with attention to structural integrity, clean anchoring, and neat aesthetics. We prioritize clear communication, punctual site measurements, and dependable after-installation responsiveness.
