Questions About Our Home Safety Services

Invisible grill installation involves fixing high-tensile, slim stainless steel cables across balconies, windows, and open areas to ensure family safety without obstructing ventilation or panoramic outdoor views.

Invisible grills can be installed on apartment balconies, bedroom windows, staircases, terrace boundaries, and open utility passages in both high-rise residential towers and standalone houses.

Yes, invisible grills are specifically designed for balconies. They provide critical fall protection for children and pets without creating the claustrophobic feeling of bulky traditional iron grilles.

Bird net installation is the fitting of durable copolymer mesh across balconies, windows, and duct areas to prevent pigeons and other birds from nesting, perching, or creating unhygienic droppings.

Yes, bird nets are custom-fitted to balconies of any size or shape to keep your space clean and hygienic without blocking fresh air or natural light.

A safety net is a reliable, heavy-duty protective netting system installed across balconies, windows, and open stairwells to provide an essential safety barrier against accidental falls for children and pets.

Safety nets can be installed on balconies, large sliding windows, internal open voids, building ducts, stairwells, and terrace perimeters.

Yes, LBC Home Safety installs durable sports nets for cricket practice pitches, football boundary nets, badminton courts, and rooftop sports setups.
